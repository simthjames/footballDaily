### The Art of Set-Pieces
Arsenal's recent success has been largely attributed to their proficiency in set-pieces. The Gunners have consistently demonstrated an ability to score crucial goals from dead-ball situations, leaving fans and pundits alike wondering if this approach is 'ugly' or 'beautiful'. While some may view set-pieces as an unattractive aspect of the game, others see it as a testament to the team's cleverness and strategy.

### Masterminds Behind the Strategy
Mikel Arteta, Arsenal's manager, has been credited with implementing this set-piece approach. The Spaniard has meticulously worked on perfecting the team's dead-ball delivery, and the results are evident. From cleverly crafted corner kicks to precision-free kicks, Arsenal's set-pieces have become a force to be reckoned with in the Premier League.

### The Numbers Game
The statistics speak for themselves. Arsenal has scored a significant number of goals from set-pieces this season, with many of these goals proving to be decisive in crucial matches. This trend has sparked a debate among football enthusiasts, with some hailing the team's set-piece expertise as a 'beautiful' aspect of their game, while others view it as 'ugly' and unattractive.
