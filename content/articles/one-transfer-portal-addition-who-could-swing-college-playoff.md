The college football season is approaching, and teams are looking to bolster their rosters through the transfer portal. With the potential to significantly impact a team's chances of making the College Football Playoff, transfer portal additions can be a game-changer.

Several top 25 teams have already made significant additions through the transfer portal, while others are still searching for the right players to fill gaps in their roster. For example, the number one ranked team, Alabama, could benefit from adding a talented receiver to complement their strong quarterback play.
