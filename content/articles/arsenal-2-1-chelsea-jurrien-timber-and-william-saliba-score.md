Arsenal have secured a crucial 2-1 victory over Chelsea in a thrilling Premier League encounter. The match was also marked by a red card for Chelsea's Pedro Neto, further complicating the Blues' chances of turning the game around.

The Emirates Stadium was abuzz as the two London giants clashed in a match that had significant implications for the top four. Arsenal, looking to solidify their position, came out strong, with their set pieces proving to be the difference maker. Timber's header from a corner gave the Gunners an early lead, setting the tone for a match that would be decided by moments of brilliance.

### Set Piece Mastery
