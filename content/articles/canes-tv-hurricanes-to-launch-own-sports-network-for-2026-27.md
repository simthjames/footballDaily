By controlling their own broadcasting platform, the team aims to provide more comprehensive coverage of their games, practices, and community events. This move is expected to foster a closer relationship between the team and its supporters, offering a more personalized viewing experience.

One of the primary goals of Canes TV is to expand the Hurricanes' reach beyond their local fanbase. With the power of modern streaming technology, fans from all over the world will have the opportunity to tune in and follow the team more closely than ever before. The creation of Canes TV involves a multifaceted approach, including the production of original content such as documentaries, analysis shows, and live game broadcasts.
