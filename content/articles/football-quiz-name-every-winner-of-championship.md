The English Football League Championship, often referred to as the Championship, is the second tier of the English football league system. ### A Brief History of the Championship
The Championship has undergone several transformations since its inception. Initially known as the Football League Second Division, it was renamed the Football League First Division in 1992, following the formation of the Premier League. The league was rebranded as the Football League Championship in 2004, adopting its current name.
