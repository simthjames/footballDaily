### Breaking Down Barriers in Sports
Unified Sports is a revolutionary program that brings together athletes with and without intellectual disabilities to compete and train as a team. This initiative has been gaining momentum worldwide, and Central New York is no exception. Recently, five coaches from the region shared their experiences and what makes Unified Sports so special.

### A Win-Win for Everyone Involved
According to the coaches, the beauty of Unified Sports lies in its ability to create a sense of community and belonging among players. 'Everybody walks away a winner' is the mantra that resonates throughout the program. The coaches highlighted the positive impact that Unified Sports has on the lives of athletes, helping them develop essential skills, build confidence, and forge lasting friendships.

### Empowering Athletes and Coaches Alike
The coaches also emphasized the importance of empowerment in Unified Sports. By providing a platform for athletes to shine, the program helps to break down social barriers and challenge traditional notions of ability and disability. As one coach noted, 'Unified Sports is not just about the game; it's about creating a sense of purpose and belonging among players.'
