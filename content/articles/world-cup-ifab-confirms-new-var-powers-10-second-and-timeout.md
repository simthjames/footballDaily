### The Future of Football: A New Era Unfolds
The International Football Association Board (IFAB) has announced major rule changes for the upcoming World Cup, set to revolutionize the beautiful game. The new rules include enhanced VAR powers, 10-second substitutions, and a ban on tactical timeouts.

### Breaking Down the Changes
The introduction of VAR has been a significant talking point in recent years, with many arguing that it has improved the accuracy of refereeing decisions. The new rules will grant VARs more power to intervene in certain situations, reducing the need for on-field referees to consult the pitch-side monitor. This change is expected to speed up the game and reduce controversy.

The 10-second substitution rule is another significant change, aimed at reducing time-wasting and increasing the pace of the game. Players will now have just 10 seconds to leave the field after being substituted, reducing the likelihood of delaying tactics.

The tactical timeout ban is perhaps the most surprising change, as it will prevent teams from taking intentional timeouts to disrupt their opponents' rhythm. This rule change is expected to improve the flow of the game and reduce the number of stoppages.
