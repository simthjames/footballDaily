### Trump Sounds the Alarm on College Sports
Former US President Donald Trump has sounded the alarm on the state of college sports, warning that the whole educational system could go out of business unless drastic changes are made. Speaking at a recent college sports roundtable, Trump emphasized the need for reform, citing the current system as unsustainable.

### A Call to Action
Trump's comments have sparked a heated debate about the future of college sports and the educational system as a whole. With the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) facing numerous challenges, including lawsuits, scandals, and declining revenues, many are calling for a comprehensive overhaul of the system. Trump's warning has added fuel to the fire, with many experts and stakeholders weighing in on the issue.

### The Current State of College Sports
The NCAA has been facing significant challenges in recent years, from player compensation and revenue sharing to recruiting scandals and academic integrity. The organization has also been criticized for its handling of player welfare, including concussion protocols and mental health support. With the rise of name, image, and likeness (NIL) deals, the lines between amateur and professional sports have become increasingly blurred, leading to concerns about the exploitation of student-athletes.

### Proposed Solutions
So far, several solutions have been proposed to address the challenges facing college sports. These include increased funding for athletic programs, improved support for student-athletes, and greater transparency and accountability within the NCAA. Some have also called for a more radical overhaul of the system, including the introduction of a pay-for-play model or the creation of a separate professional league for college-aged athletes.
