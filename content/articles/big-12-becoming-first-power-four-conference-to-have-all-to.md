### Unprecedented Unity
The Big 12 Conference has made history by becoming the first Power Four conference to have all its members agree to participate in the College Sports Council (CSC). This unanimous decision marks a significant milestone in college athletics, as it paves the way for a more unified and collaborative approach to governance.

### A New Era in College Sports
The Big 12 Conference's decision to participate in the CSC is a testament to the conference's commitment to innovation and progress. By working together, the member institutions can share best practices, address common challenges, and create new opportunities for student-athletes. This move is expected to have a profound impact on the future of college sports, as it sets a precedent for other conferences to follow.

### What Does This Mean for College Athletics?
The implications of the Big 12 Conference's decision are far-reaching. With all member institutions on board, the conference can now work towards creating a more streamlined and efficient system for governing college sports. This, in turn, can lead to improved outcomes for student-athletes, enhanced fan experiences, and increased revenue for the conference.
