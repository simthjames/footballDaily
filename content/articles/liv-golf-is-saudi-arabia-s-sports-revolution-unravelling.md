### LIV Golf: The Rise and Potential Fall

This development has raised questions about the sustainability of the tour and the broader implications for Saudi Arabia's sports revolution.

The LIV Golf tour was launched in 2022 with the aim of rivalling the established PGA Tour and European Tour.
