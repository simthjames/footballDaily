### Breaking News: Rangers Complete Signing of Ben Godfrey
Rangers have confirmed the signing of former Everton defender Ben Godfrey on a season-long loan deal from Italian side Atalanta. The highly anticipated move is expected to bolster the Scottish club's defensive lineup for the upcoming season.

Godfrey, who has previously played for England's youth teams, joined Atalanta from Everton in the summer but has found playing time limited. The move to Rangers offers him a fresh start and an opportunity to regain his form.
