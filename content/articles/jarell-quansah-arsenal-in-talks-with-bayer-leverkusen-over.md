Quansah, known for his versatility and defensive prowess, could be the missing piece in Arsenal's lineup. With his ability to play in various positions, he could provide Arteta with the flexibility to experiment with different formations and tactics.

Mikel Arteta has been vocal about his desire to strengthen Arsenal's defense, and the signing of Quansah could be a significant step in that direction. With Quansah on board, Arteta could finally have the defensive solidity he needs to push for a top-four finish.
