### The Azteca Altitude Conundrum
The Estadio Azteca, home of the Mexican national football team, is one of the most iconic and intimidating venues in world football. With a seating capacity of over 87,000, it's a cauldron of noise and passion that can overwhelm even the most experienced teams. However, it's not just the fervent atmosphere that poses a challenge to visiting teams - the stadium's high altitude is a significant factor that can leave players gasping for air.

### Physical Demands of High Altitude
The Azteca Stadium is situated at an altitude of 2,240 meters (7,350 feet) above sea level, which can cause a range of physical problems for players who are not accustomed to training and playing at such heights. The lower air pressure and reduced oxygen levels can lead to fatigue, dizziness, and shortness of breath, making it difficult for players to perform at their best. As one player put it, 'You can't catch your breath' - a sentiment that echoes the sentiments of many who have played at the Azteca.

### England's Preparation
So, how will England prepare for the challenges of playing at the Azteca? The team's coaching staff will likely employ a range of strategies to acclimatize the players to the high altitude, including tailored training sessions and carefully planned travel arrangements. The players will also be advised on how to manage their breathing and conserve energy during the match, as well as how to stay hydrated and fueled throughout the 90 minutes.
