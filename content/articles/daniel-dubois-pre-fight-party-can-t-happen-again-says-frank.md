Daniel Dubois is gearing up for his highly anticipated world title clash against Fabio Wardley, and promoter Frank Warren has sent a clear message: the pre-fight party that preceded Dubois' previous bout cannot happen again. Warren's emphasis on focus and discipline underscores the high stakes of this upcoming fight.

Dubois' pre-fight party ahead of his last bout raised eyebrows, with many questioning whether such celebrations were premature. Warren has made it clear that this kind of distraction cannot be afforded in the lead-up to the Wardley clash. The focus must be solely on the fight, with every aspect of Dubois' training and preparation scrutinized to ensure he is in the best possible shape.

### The Opponent
Fabio Wardley presents a formidable challenge for Dubois. With a strong record and a reputation for being a tough opponent, Wardley will push Dubois to his limits. The world title clash promises to be an exciting encounter, with both fighters eager to prove themselves.
