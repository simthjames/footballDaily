### The Stage is Set
The Champions League has reached its knockout phase, and one of the most highly anticipated matches of the round of 16 is about to unfold. Real Madrid, the Spanish giants, are set to host Manchester City, the English powerhouse, in the first leg of their Champions League tie. ### A Clash of Styles
Real Madrid and Manchester City have distinct styles of play that will make for a fascinating clash. Real Madrid, under the management of Carlo Ancelotti, have been known for their attacking flair and ability to score crucial goals in big matches. Manchester City, on the other hand, have been a well-oiled machine under Pep Guardiola, with a strong emphasis on possession football and pressing high up the pitch.

### Past Encounters
This will not be the first time Real Madrid and Manchester City have faced each other in the Champions League. Their most recent encounter was in the 2021-22 season, when Manchester City edged out Real Madrid in the semifinals, winning 4-3 on aggregate.
