The Colorado school district has pushed back against claims made by the Department of Education regarding its athletic teams. In response, the school district has issued an impasse letter, formally disputing the claims and requesting a resolution to the matter.

The dispute centers around the district's allocation of resources for athletic teams, with the Department of Education alleging that the district has failed to provide adequate support for certain teams. However, the school district maintains that it has acted in accordance with all relevant regulations and guidelines.
