Caitlin Clark, the talented Fever star, has torched officials after receiving a technical foul for 'clapping' during a recent game. The incident has sparked widespread debate, with many calling into question the inconsistency of refereeing decisions.

Clark's frustration is understandable, given the seemingly minor infraction that led to the technical foul. While clapping is generally considered a harmless expression of enthusiasm, in this case, it was deemed worthy of a technical foul.
