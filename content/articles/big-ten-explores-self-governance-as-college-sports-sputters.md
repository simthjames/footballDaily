The Big Ten is exploring the possibility of self-governance as the College Sports Commission struggles to gain traction and Congress action stalls. This move could significantly impact the future of college athletics, allowing the Big Ten to have more control over its own affairs.

### Background on the College Sports Commission
The College Sports Commission was established to oversee and regulate college athletics, but it has faced numerous challenges and setbacks. With the lack of progress, the Big Ten is considering taking matters into its own hands.

### Potential Benefits of Self-Governance
Self-governance could provide the Big Ten with more autonomy and flexibility to make decisions that are in the best interest of its member schools. This could include setting its own rules and regulations, as well as having more control over revenue distribution.
