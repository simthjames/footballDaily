### A Rivalry Born from Defeat
The infamous phrase 'Your boys took a hell of a beating' still echoes in the minds of English football fans, a stark reminder of the 2-1 defeat at the hands of Norway in 1981. This loss marked the beginning of a longstanding rivalry between the two nations, with each side seeking to outdo the other on the pitch.

### Historical Context
The 1981 match was more than just a simple defeat for England; it was a wake-up call. The Norwegian team, led by Coach Tor Røste Fossen, employed an unorthodox strategy that caught the English off guard. This victory was not just a testament to Norway's skill but also a demonstration of their cunning and adaptability on the field.

### The Road to Redemption
Since then, England has sought to redeem themselves, but Norway has consistently proven to be a formidable opponent.
