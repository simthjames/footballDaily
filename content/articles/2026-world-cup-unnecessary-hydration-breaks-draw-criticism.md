### Kicking Off the Controversy
The 2026 World Cup has been making headlines for all the wrong reasons, with the latest controversy surrounding 'unnecessary' hydration breaks. Players, coaches, and fans alike have been vocal about their dissatisfaction with the frequent breaks, claiming they disrupt the flow of the game.

### The Rule in Question
According to the new regulations, hydration breaks are mandatory in matches where the temperature exceeds a certain threshold. However, many have argued that the breaks are being taken too frequently, and in some cases, when the temperature is not excessively high. This has led to frustration among players and coaches, who feel that the breaks are affecting the momentum of the game.

### Player and Coach Reactions
Several high-profile players and coaches have spoken out against the hydration breaks, labeling them 'unnecessary' and 'disruptive'. Some have even suggested that the breaks are being used as an excuse for players to take a breather, rather than being genuinely needed for hydration purposes.

### The Impact on the Game
The controversy surrounding hydration breaks has sparked a wider debate about the impact of external factors on the game. ### A Solution in Sight?
