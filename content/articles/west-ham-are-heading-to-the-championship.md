West Ham United's struggles in the Premier League have finally caught up with them, as they face the harsh reality of relegation to the Championship. ### The Road to Relegation
So, what went wrong for West Ham United? The team's poor defensive record was a major contributing factor to their relegation. The departure of key players and the failure to replace them adequately also had a significant impact on the team's performance.
