### Positional Battles

The goalkeeping spot, defensive lineup, midfield composition, and forward selection all present Tuchel with difficult decisions. The writers' suggestions for each area of the team highlight the depth of talent available but also underscore the challenges of selecting a squad that can meet the diverse demands of the World Cup.
