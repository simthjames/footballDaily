### Cleaning Up the Transfer Market
The world of sports has been plagued by the issue of 'ghost transfers' for far too long. These illicit transactions, where players are transferred between teams without proper documentation or oversight, have led to a lack of transparency and fairness in the transfer market. However, in a bid to tackle this problem, the DI Cabinet has adopted new rules to address ghost transfers in all sports.

### What are Ghost Transfers?
Ghost transfers refer to the practice of transferring players between teams without properly registering the transaction with the relevant governing bodies. This can lead to a range of issues, including tax evasion, money laundering, and the exploitation of players. The lack of transparency in these transactions also makes it difficult to track the movement of players and ensure that teams are complying with regulations.

### New Rules to Prevent Ghost Transfers
The new rules adopted by the DI Cabinet aim to prevent ghost transfers by introducing stricter regulations and oversight mechanisms. These rules include the requirement for all transfer transactions to be properly documented and registered with the relevant governing bodies. Teams will also be required to provide detailed information about the transfer, including the player's identity, the transfer fee, and the terms of the contract.
