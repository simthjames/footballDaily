Michigan's recent NCAA championship win has sent shockwaves throughout the college basketball world, and at the heart of this success is Coach Dusty May's unconventional yet effective strategy of building a 'monster portal team'. In an era where the NCAA's transfer portal has become a crucial tool for roster construction, May has demonstrated a unique understanding of how to navigate and exploit this system to assemble a team capable of dominating on the biggest stage.

The concept of a 'portal team' refers to a roster primarily composed of players who have transferred from other colleges, utilizing the NCAA's transfer portal. This approach allows coaches to bypass traditional recruiting methods, focusing instead on proven college players looking for a new opportunity. May's mastery of this strategy has been hailed as a key factor in Michigan's championship run, with many pundits and fans alike praising his ability to identify, recruit, and integrate top talent from across the country.

### The Art of Building a Portal Team
Building a successful portal team requires a deep understanding of the transfer market, an ability to evaluate talent, and the capacity to create a cohesive unit from disparate parts. May's success in this area can be attributed to his meticulous approach, which involves identifying players who not only fit Michigan's system but also complement the existing roster. This careful planning has allowed Michigan to field a team that is greater than the sum of its parts, with each player contributing to a cohesive and formidable unit.

### The Impact of the Transfer Portal
The NCAA's transfer portal has revolutionized the way college basketball teams are built. No longer are coaches limited to recruiting high school players or waiting for transfers to become eligible. The portal provides a platform for players to explore new opportunities and for coaches to address specific needs on their roster. May's use of the portal to build Michigan's championship team is a testament to the potential of this system, showcasing how it can be harnessed to achieve success at the highest level.
