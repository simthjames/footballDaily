Arsenal has officially opened talks with England midfielder Declan Rice regarding a potential new contract. This development has sent shockwaves of excitement through the Gunners fanbase, as they envision the versatile and skilled player donning their beloved team's jersey.

Rice's ability to read the game, intercept passes, and launch counterattacks would be a perfect fit for Arsenal's high-pressing, attacking style of play. Moreover, his experience playing for England would bring an added layer of leadership and big-game mentality to the team.
