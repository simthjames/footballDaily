### What are Prediction Markets?
Prediction markets are platforms that allow users to bet on the outcome of various events, including sports. These markets are often seen as a way for people to make informed decisions based on data and analysis. However, they have also been criticized for their potential to compromise the integrity of sports.

### The Concerns Over Sports Betting
Sports betting has been a topic of debate for a long time, with many arguing that it can lead to match-fixing and other forms of corruption. The introduction of prediction markets has further complicated the issue, as it allows users to bet on specific outcomes within a game. This has raised concerns over the potential for insider trading and other forms of manipulation.
