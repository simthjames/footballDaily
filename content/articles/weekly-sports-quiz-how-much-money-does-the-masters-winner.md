### The Ultimate Prize in Golf
The Masters is one of the most prestigious golf tournaments in the world, attracting top players and fans alike. But have you ever wondered how much money the winner of this esteemed tournament takes home? The answer might surprise you.

The Masters winner's purse is a staggering amount, one that can change the life of a professional golfer overnight. With a total prize fund of over $15 million, the winner's share is the largest in golf. But what exactly does the winner get, and how does it compare to other major golf tournaments?

### A Brief History of the Masters
The Masters has a rich history, dating back to 1934 when it was first founded by Bobby Jones and Clifford Roberts. Since then, it has become one of the most iconic golf tournaments in the world, with a reputation for excellence and tradition. From the famous green jacket to the picturesque Augusta National Golf Club, the Masters is a true test of golfing skill and endurance.

### The Prize Money Breakdown
So, how much money does the Masters winner actually get? The answer is a whopping $2.7 million, which is the largest share of the total prize fund. But that's not all - the winner also receives a number of other benefits, including the famous green jacket, a lifetime exemption to the Masters, and a five-year exemption to the other major golf championships.
