### Ripples in College Basketball
College basketball is also likely to feel the repercussions of this eligibility change. The NBA's "one-and-done" rule, which requires players to be at least 19 years old and one year removed from high school to be eligible for the draft, could be indirectly affected. However, this change could also create challenges for college basketball programs, as they might need to adapt their recruiting strategies and roster management to accommodate returning athletes.
