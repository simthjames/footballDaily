### Introduction to the 2026 College Football Season
The 2026 college football season is fast approaching, and with it comes the usual speculation about which coaches are on the hot seat. With 138 FBS coaches in the mix, there's no shortage of storylines to follow. From powerhouses like Alabama and Clemson to smaller schools like Appalachian State and Liberty, every program has its own unique set of challenges and expectations.

### The Art of Coaching in College Football
Coaching in college football is a high-pressure job, with fans, alumni, and administrators all having high expectations for success. A single losing season can be enough to put a coach on the hot seat, and the constant turnover can be dizzying. But what makes a coach successful? Is it their ability to recruit top talent, their mastery of the X's and O's, or their ability to motivate their players?

### Evaluating Job Security
Evaluating the job security of FBS coaches is a complex task, with many factors to consider. A coach's win-loss record is obviously a key factor, but it's not the only one. Other considerations include their ability to recruit top talent, their success in conference play, and their ability to develop players for the NFL. Some coaches are able to overcome a poor win-loss record with strong recruiting classes and a promising young roster, while others may be shown the door despite a decent record due to off-field issues or a lack of progress.

### The Impact of Recruiting on Job Security
Recruiting is a critical component of college football, and a coach's ability to bring in top talent can make or break their job security. Coaches who consistently land top recruits are often able to overcome a poor win-loss record, at least in the short term. On the other hand, coaches who struggle to recruit may find themselves on the hot seat, even if they have a decent record. The ability to develop players for the NFL is also a key factor, as it not only helps to attract top recruits but also demonstrates a coach's ability to prepare players for the next level.

### The Role of Administration in Coaching Decisions
Administrators play a crucial role in coaching decisions, and their expectations can vary widely from school to school. Some administrators may prioritize winning above all else, while others may place a greater emphasis on factors like academics, player development, and community engagement. Coaches who are able to balance these competing demands are often more likely to keep their jobs, even in the face of a poor win-loss record.

### Coaching Carousel
The coaching carousel is always in motion, with coaches being hired and fired on a regular basis. Some coaches are able to navigate this landscape with ease, landing new jobs quickly and seamlessly. Others may struggle to find new employment, particularly if they have a poor track record or have been out of the game for an extended period. The ability to adapt to changing circumstances and to market oneself effectively is critical for coaches looking to stay employed.
