### Understanding the NIL Deals
The NIL deals in question are third-party agreements that allow college athletes to profit from their name, image, and likeness. These deals can be lucrative, with top athletes earning significant amounts of money from endorsement contracts, autograph signings, and other business ventures. However, the CSC has strict guidelines in place to ensure that these deals do not compromise the integrity of college sports.
