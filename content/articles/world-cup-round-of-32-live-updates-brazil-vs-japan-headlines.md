Brazil's attacking trio of Neymar, Vinicius Jr., and Richarlison will look to exploit Japan's defense with their speed and skill. However, the Japanese team has been working on their defensive solidity, and it will be interesting to see how they cope with the Brazilian attack. On the other hand, Japan's midfield duo of Hidemasa Morita and Wataru Endo will try to control the tempo of the game and create scoring opportunities for their team.

### History of Encounters
Brazil and Japan have met each other five times in the past, with Brazil winning four of those matches. However, Japan has been improving steadily over the years, and they will be looking to cause an upset against the favorites. The last time these two teams met was in 2013, when Brazil won 3-0 in a friendly match.

For Japan, the player to watch will be Takumi Minamino, who has been impressive with his speed and agility on the wing.
