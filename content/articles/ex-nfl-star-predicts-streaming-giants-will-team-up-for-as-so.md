### The Changing Landscape of Sports Streaming
The world of sports streaming is on the cusp of a significant shift, with the National Football League (NFL) at the forefront. A former NFL star has made a prediction that could change the way we consume sports content: streaming giants will team up to offer bundles as the cost of league rights becomes prohibitively expensive.

The NFL is one of the most lucrative sports leagues in the world, with a massive following and astronomical broadcasting rights deals. However, the increasing cost of these rights is forcing streaming services to rethink their strategies. With the likes of Amazon Prime, Apple TV+, and Disney+ vying for a piece of the NFL pie, the market is becoming increasingly crowded and expensive.

### The Rise of Bundling
Bundling is not a new concept in the world of streaming. Services like Netflix and Hulu have long offered bundles that include multiple channels and services for a discounted price. However, in the context of sports streaming, bundling could take on a new meaning. Imagine being able to access multiple sports leagues and events through a single subscription, with the option to add or remove channels as you see fit.

This is the future of sports streaming, according to the ex-NFL star. With the cost of individual league rights becoming 'so damn expensive,' streaming services will be forced to team up and offer bundles to consumers. This could lead to a more streamlined and affordable experience for sports fans, who will no longer have to shell out for multiple separate subscriptions.

### The Implications for Sports Fans
So, what does this mean for sports fans? In the short term, it could mean more options and better value for money. With multiple streaming services teaming up to offer bundles, consumers will have more choice and flexibility when it comes to accessing their favorite sports leagues and events.

However, there are also potential downsides to consider. With more services teaming up, there is a risk that consumers will be forced to pay for content they don't want or need. This could lead to a more complicated and expensive experience for sports fans, who may find themselves paying for a bundle that includes channels they never watch.

### The Future of Sports Streaming
As the world of sports streaming continues to evolve, it's clear that the NFL will play a major role. With its massive following and lucrative broadcasting rights deals, the NFL is a prize that many streaming services are eager to get their hands on.

But as the cost of league rights becomes increasingly expensive, it's likely that we'll see more streaming services teaming up to offer bundles. This could lead to a more streamlined and affordable experience for sports fans, but it also raises important questions about the future of sports streaming.

Will we see a return to the old days of traditional broadcasting, where consumers are forced to pay for a bundle of channels they may not want or need? Or will the rise of bundling lead to a more flexible and consumer-friendly experience, where sports fans can pick and choose the content they want to watch?

Only time will tell, but one thing is certain: the world of sports streaming is on the cusp of a significant shift, and the NFL is at the forefront.
