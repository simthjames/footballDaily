### Shakeup in Ann Arbor
The University of Michigan athletic department is set to undergo a significant change as Athletic Director Warde Manuel has announced his decision to step down from his position at the end of the calendar year. This move is expected to have far-reaching implications for the Wolverines' athletic programs.

### A Decade of Leadership
Manuel has been at the helm of Michigan's athletic department since 2016, overseeing numerous successes across various sports. His tenure has been marked by the football team's resurgence, the men's basketball team's consistent competitiveness, and the women's basketball team's growth. Manuel's leadership has also seen significant investments in facilities and resources, enhancing the overall student-athlete experience.

### Challenges and Achievements
During his time as Athletic Director, Manuel has navigated the department through challenges such as the COVID-19 pandemic, conference realignments, and increasing competitive pressures. Despite these challenges, Michigan has maintained its stature as a powerhouse in college athletics, with teams regularly competing for and winning conference and national championships.

### The Road Ahead
As Manuel prepares to depart, the focus shifts to finding his successor. The university will embark on a search for a new athletic director, looking for a leader who can build on the foundation laid by Manuel and propel Michigan's athletic programs to even greater heights. The next athletic director will face the task of balancing tradition with innovation, ensuring that Michigan remains competitive in an ever-changing collegiate athletic landscape.
