### Coaching Chaos in the NHL
The New York Islanders have made a shocking move by firing head coach Patrick Roy with just four games remaining before the playoffs. This decision comes as a surprise to many in the hockey world, given the timing and the team's current position in the standings.

### A Change in Leadership
The Islanders have announced that Peter DeBoer will take over as the new head coach, replacing Roy. DeBoer is a seasoned coach with a wealth of experience in the NHL, having previously coached the Florida Panthers, New Jersey Devils, and San Jose Sharks. He is known for his ability to get the most out of his players and make adjustments on the fly.
