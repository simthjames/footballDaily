The World Cup is just around the corner, and the football world is abuzz with excitement. As teams begin to announce their squads, there's a lot of speculation about who will make the cut. For England, one of the most talked-about positions is the left-back spot, with Luke Shaw being a strong contender.

Former Manchester United midfielder Michael Carrick has come out in support of Shaw, backing him for a place in the England squad. Shaw has had his fair share of ups and downs throughout his career.
