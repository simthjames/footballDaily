### The Shift in College Sports Landscape
The world of college sports has witnessed a significant transformation in recent times, with the expansion frenzy taking center stage. This shift has brought about both winners and losers, as teams and conferences navigate the changing landscape.

### Winners in the Expansion Frenzy
The expansion of the NCAA Tournament field is a welcome move for many teams and conferences. ### The Role of the CFP in College Sports
