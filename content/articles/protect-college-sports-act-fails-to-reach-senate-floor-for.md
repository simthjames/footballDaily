### Senate Inaction on College Sports Act
The Protect College Sports Act has failed to reach the Senate floor for a vote, as lawmakers have recessed until September. This development comes as a significant setback for the legislation, which aimed to address various issues affecting college sports.

### Background and Implications
The Protect College Sports Act was introduced to regulate college athletics, particularly in the context of player compensation and revenue distribution. The bill's failure to reach the Senate floor for a vote means that these issues will remain unaddressed for the time being. This could have far-reaching implications for college sports, including the potential for further legal challenges and uncertainties for student-athletes.

### Lawmakers' Recess and Future Prospects
