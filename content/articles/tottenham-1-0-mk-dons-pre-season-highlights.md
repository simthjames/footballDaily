### Spurs Kick Off Pre-Season with a Win
Tottenham Hotspur commenced their pre-season campaign with a narrow 1-0 victory over MK Dons. The match, which took place at the Stadium MK, saw the Spurs side dominated possession but struggle to find the back of the net against a resolute MK Dons defense.

### First Half Highlights
The first half was a cagey affair, with both teams creating half-chances but neither able to capitalize. Tottenham's new signings were on full display, with some impressive individual performances. However, it was the home side that came closest to scoring in the opening 45 minutes, with a series of dangerous set pieces causing concern for the Spurs defense.

### Second Half Turning Point
The second half saw Tottenham increase the pressure, and their persistence eventually paid off. A well-crafted move down the left flank ended with a precision cross into the box, which was met by a towering header from one of the Spurs attackers. The goal, which came just after the hour mark, proved to be the decisive moment in the match.

### Post-Match Reaction
Following the game, Tottenham manager Antonio Conte expressed his satisfaction with the team's performance, highlighting the importance of these pre-season friendlies in building fitness and cohesion. He also praised the opposition, acknowledging that MK Dons had provided a stern test for his side.
