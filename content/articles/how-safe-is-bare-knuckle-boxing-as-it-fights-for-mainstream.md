### The Rise of a Brutal Sport
Bare-knuckle boxing, a sport often associated with the early days of boxing, is making a comeback. The question on everyone's mind is: how safe is it? As the sport fights for mainstream appeal, concerns about the safety of its fighters are growing. With its brutal and intense nature, bare-knuckle boxing is a far cry from the heavily regulated and padded world of traditional boxing.

### A Lack of Protection
One of the main concerns surrounding bare-knuckle boxing is the lack of protection for its fighters. Without gloves, the risk of cuts, bruises, and broken bones increases significantly. The sport's governing bodies argue that the lack of gloves actually reduces the number of punches thrown, as fighters are more cautious about the damage they can inflict. However, this argument is not convincing to many, who believe that the risks outweigh any potential benefits.

### The Physical Toll
The physical toll of bare-knuckle boxing is undeniable. Fighters are at risk of serious injury, including concussions, broken hands, and facial lacerations. The sport's most high-profile promoter, David Feldman, has acknowledged the risks, but argues that the rewards are worth it. Feldman claims that bare-knuckle boxing is a more honest and pure form of the sport, with fighters forced to rely on their skills rather than their ability to take a punch.

### The Mental Health Impact
The mental health impact of bare-knuckle boxing is also a concern. The sport's intense and brutal nature can take a toll on a fighter's mental wellbeing, with many reporting feelings of anxiety and depression. The lack of support for fighters' mental health is a worrying trend, with many promoters and governing bodies prioritizing the sport's physical aspects over its fighters' mental wellbeing.

### The Fight for Mainstream Appeal
Despite the concerns surrounding its safety, bare-knuckle boxing is fighting for mainstream appeal. The sport has gained a significant following in recent years, with many fans drawn to its raw and unfiltered nature. However, as the sport continues to grow, it is essential that its governing bodies prioritize the safety and wellbeing of its fighters. This includes providing adequate medical care, supporting fighters' mental health, and implementing rules to reduce the risk of injury.

### The Future of Bare-Knuckle Boxing
The future of bare-knuckle boxing is uncertain. While the sport has gained a significant following, its safety concerns and lack of regulation are major obstacles to its growth. As the sport continues to fight for mainstream appeal, it is essential that its governing bodies prioritize the safety and wellbeing of its fighters. This includes working with medical professionals to develop safer rules and regulations, and providing support for fighters' mental health.

### The Bottom Line
The safety of bare-knuckle boxing is a concern that cannot be ignored. As the sport fights for mainstream appeal, it is essential that its governing bodies prioritize the safety and wellbeing of its fighters. Only by prioritizing the safety of its fighters can bare-knuckle boxing hope to achieve mainstream success.
