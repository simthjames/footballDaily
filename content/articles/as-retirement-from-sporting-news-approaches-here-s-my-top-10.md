As I approach my retirement from Sporting News, I couldn't help but think about the countless memories I've made over the years covering the world of sports. In this article, I'll take you through my top 10 Final Four memories, a journey through time that highlights the excitement, drama, and unforgettable moments that have made the Final Four a beloved tradition.

**The Stage is Set: Introduction to the Final Four**
The Final Four is more than just a sporting event; it's an experience that captivates audiences nationwide. The road to the Final Four is paved with upsets, buzzer-beaters, and Cinderella stories, each contributing to the rich tapestry of college basketball lore. From the opening tip of the first game to the final whistle of the championship, the Final Four is a rollercoaster of emotions, filled with moments that etch themselves into the memories of fans and players alike.

**Counting Down the Memories: The Top 10**
1. **2019: Virginia's Redemption** - The Cavaliers' first NCAA title, coming a year after their historic first-round loss to UMBC, stands as a testament to perseverance and determination.
2. **2018: Loyola-Chicago's Magical Run** - The Ramblers' unexpected journey to the Final Four, led by Sister Jean, captured the hearts of many and reminded us of the power of underdog stories in sports.
3. **2017: North Carolina's Sixth Title** - The Tar Heels' victory over Gonzaga marked a moment of triumph for a program with a rich history of success.
4. **2016: Villanova's Buzzer-Beater** - Kris Jenkins' game-winning three-pointer against North Carolina is forever etched in Final Four history as one of the most thrilling conclusions to a championship game.
5. **2015: Duke's Fifth Title** - The Blue Devils' win, led by freshmen Jahlil Okafor, Tyus Jones, and Grayson Allen, showcased the future of basketball talent.
6. **2014: UConn's Seventh Seed Miracle** - The Huskies' run from a seventh seed to the championship demonstrated the unpredictability and excitement of the NCAA tournament.
7. **2013: Louisville's Redemption** - The Cardinals' victory, after the tragic loss of a teammate, was a heartfelt moment of triumph over adversity.
8. **2012: Kentucky's Dominance** - The Wildcats' championship, led by Anthony Davis, highlighted the one-and-done era's impact on college basketball.
9. **2011: UConn's Kemba Walker** - Walker's incredible tournament performance, leading the Huskies to the title, is remembered as one of the greatest individual feats in Final Four history.
10. **2010: Duke's Fourth Title** - The Blue Devils' win over Butler marked a moment of resurgence for a storied program, demonstrating the enduring power of tradition in college basketball.

These memories, among many others, have made my career covering the Final Four an enriching and unforgettable experience.
