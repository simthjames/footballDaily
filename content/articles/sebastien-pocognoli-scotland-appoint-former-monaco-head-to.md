### A New Era Begins
The Scotland national team has appointed Sebastien Pocognoli as their new head coach, replacing Steve Clarke on a two-year contract. This move marks a significant change in the team's leadership, with Pocognoli bringing his experience from his time as the head coach of Monaco.

### Pocognoli's Background
Pocognoli has a rich history in football, having played for several clubs during his career. He has also had stints as a coach, most notably with Monaco, where he gained valuable experience that will undoubtedly benefit the Scotland national team.

### What to Expect
