The world of sports betting is on the cusp of a significant transformation, as a new bill aims to regulate sports betting on prediction markets. ### The Current State of Affairs
Currently, sports betting on prediction markets operates in a grey area, with many platforms offering betting services without proper oversight. The new bill seeks to address these concerns by establishing a regulatory framework that will govern the industry and ensure that all operators are held to the same standards.

### Key Provisions of the Bill
The bill proposes several key provisions, including the creation of a regulatory body that will oversee the industry, the establishment of strict licensing requirements for operators, and the implementation of robust consumer protection measures.
