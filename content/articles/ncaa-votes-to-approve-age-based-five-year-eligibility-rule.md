### Impact on College Football
The age-based five-year eligibility rule is expected to have a profound impact on college football. Coaches and teams will need to adapt their recruitment strategies, taking into account the extended eligibility period. ### Basketball Landscape Shifts
The new eligibility rule will also have a significant impact on college basketball. The sport has long been characterized by a 'one-and-done' culture, with top players often leaving for the professional leagues after just one year of collegiate competition. The age-based five-year eligibility rule could lead to a shift away from this culture, with players opting to stay in college for longer and develop their skills further.

This, in turn, could lead to a more competitive and exciting collegiate basketball scene, with teams boasting more experienced and developed players.
