### Golfing Giants Stumble
Rory McIlroy, one of the most renowned golfers in the world, has made a woeful start at the FedEx St. Jude Championship, kicking off the tournament with a disappointing four-over par opening round. This unexpected turn of events has left many in the golfing community stunned, as McIlroy is typically known for his precision and skill on the green.

### Understanding the FedExCup Playoffs
For those who are new to the world of golf, the FedExCup Playoffs are a series of tournaments that take place at the end of the PGA Tour season. These playoffs determine the champion of the FedEx Cup, with points awarded to players based on their performance in each tournament. The player with the most points at the end of the playoffs is crowned the champion, making every round crucial in the quest for the title.

### McIlroy's Performance
McIlroy's four-over par opening round is significantly higher than what many had anticipated, given his impressive track record in previous tournaments. Despite his experience and skill, McIlroy struggled to find his rhythm, with several mishits and missed putts contributing to his high score. As the tournament progresses, it will be interesting to see how McIlroy bounces back from this setback, and whether he can regain his footing to contend for the championship.
