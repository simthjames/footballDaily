### Turning the Tables
In a shocking turn of events, Cleveland Cavaliers coach Kenny Atkinson has come out and said that his team is 'analytically' winning their series against the New York Knicks, despite being on the verge of being swept. This statement has left many in the basketball world scratching their heads, wondering what exactly Atkinson means by this claim.

### The Numbers Game
Atkinson's assertion is likely based on advanced statistics and metrics that suggest the Cavaliers are outperforming the Knicks in certain areas, such as rebounding or defensive efficiency. However, with the series currently standing at 3-0 in favor of the Knicks, it's difficult to see how the Cavaliers can be considered 'winners' in any sense of the word.

### A Desperate Attempt to Boost Morale?
Some have speculated that Atkinson's comments are an attempt to boost the morale of his team, which has struggled to find its footing in the series. By focusing on the analytical aspects of the game, Atkinson may be trying to distract from the fact that the Cavaliers are on the brink of elimination.

### What the Stats Say
A closer look at the numbers reveals that the Cavaliers have indeed been competitive in certain areas, such as three-point shooting and fast break points. However, the Knicks have dominated in other key categories, including points in the paint and rebounds. It's unclear how Atkinson is weighing these different metrics to arrive at his conclusion.
