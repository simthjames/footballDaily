The National Football League (NFL) is under scrutiny as the Department of Justice (DOJ) has launched an antitrust investigation into the league's television deals. This move has sent shockwaves throughout the sports world, with many speculating about the potential consequences.

The investigation is likely to examine the NFL's use of exclusive contracts, which prevent other networks from broadcasting NFL games.
