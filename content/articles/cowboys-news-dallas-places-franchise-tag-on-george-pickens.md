### What Does the Franchise Tag Mean?
For those unfamiliar with the intricacies of NFL contracts, the franchise tag is a designation given to a player by their team, which restricts the player's ability to become a free agent. The tag can be applied in different forms, including the non-exclusive franchise tag, which allows the player to negotiate with other teams but gives the original team the right to match any offer or receive two first-round draft picks as compensation.
