### The Psychology of Red Cards
Red cards can have a profound psychological impact on players and teams. The loss of a key player can demoralize a team, while the opposing team may gain a psychological boost. Referees must carefully consider the consequences of their decisions, as a red card can be a turning point in the match.

The FIFA World Cup is the most-watched sporting event globally, and the actions of referees are under intense scrutiny. As the tournament progresses, it will be interesting to see if the trend of red cards continues and how teams adapt to the stricter refereeing standards.
