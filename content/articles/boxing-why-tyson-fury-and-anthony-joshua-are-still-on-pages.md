### The Battle for Supremacy
The rivalry between Fury and Joshua is more than just about winning a fight; it's about establishing supremacy in the boxing world. Both fighters have a strong claim to being the best heavyweight boxer in the world, and a win against the other would cement their status as the top dog. This has led to a game of cat and mouse, with each side trying to gain the upper hand in negotiations.

### The Money Factor
One of the main sticking points in the negotiations has been the issue of money. Both Fury and Joshua are keen to maximize their earnings from the fight, and their respective teams have been locked in a battle over the purse split. Fury's team has been pushing for a 60-40 split in their favor, while Joshua's team has been advocating for a more even split.

### The Venue Conundrum
Another area of contention has been the venue for the fight. Fury's team has been pushing for the fight to take place in the UK, while Joshua's team has been keen to stage the bout in a more neutral location, such as the Middle East. The venue can have a significant impact on the atmosphere and the crowd, and both sides want to ensure that they have the advantage in this regard.

### The Road to Nowhere

The back-and-forth between the two camps has been well-documented, with each side accusing the other of being inflexible and unreasonable. It's starting to look like the fight may never happen, which would be a huge disappointment for boxing fans around the world.
